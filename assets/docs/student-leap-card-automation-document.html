Student Leap Card RPA Automation

UiPath, CSV automation, rule validation and email notifications

Summary: A UiPath automation that validates Student Leap Card applications, applies rule-based checks, writes CSV audit outputs and sends personalised email notifications.

Manual Process

The manual process required staff to open each applicant folder, read applicant_data.csv, calculate age, validate academic year, check payment status, inspect selfie matching score, update output sheets and send emails manually.

Automation Flow

  • Loop through applicant folders.
  • Read applicant CSV data.
  • Calculate age from date of birth.
  • Validate academic year and payment status.
  • Apply selfie score thresholds.
  • Classify the record as approved, manual review or rejected.
  • Write output CSV files and send email notifications.

Validation Rules

  • Age must be between 17 and 25.
  • Academic year must match the expected year.
  • Payment must be authorised.
  • Selfie score below 0.70 is rejected.
  • Selfie score between 0.70 and 0.85 is sent to manual review.
  • Selfie score above 0.85 is approved if all other checks pass.

Outcome

The bot produced consistent decisions, audit-friendly CSV files and automated communication. The project demonstrates RPA process analysis, rule automation and business value calculation.
