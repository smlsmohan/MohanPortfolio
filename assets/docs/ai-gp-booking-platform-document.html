AI GP Booking Platform Concept

AI for healthcare, NLP chatbot, appointment booking and no-show prediction

Summary: A healthcare appointment booking concept for Ireland that combines GP search, booking, reminders, chatbot support and predictive no-show analysis.
NLPbooking assistant
Eircodelocation search
MLno-show prediction

Problem

Patients often struggle to find suitable GP appointments, understand availability and manage booking changes. Clinics also lose capacity due to no-shows and manual coordination.

Solution

  • Patient-facing app for GP search and appointment booking.
  • Real-time appointment availability and cancellation flow.
  • NLP chatbot for booking help and common questions.
  • Eircode-based location filtering.
  • Reminder notifications to reduce missed appointments.
  • Machine learning concept to identify no-show risk.

Architecture

The proposed design includes a Flutter front end, Flask backend, SQL database, Rasa chatbot and machine learning prediction component.

Value

The concept demonstrates how AI can support healthcare access, reduce administrative friction and improve appointment utilisation.
